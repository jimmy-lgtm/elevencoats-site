Why Paradise Valley homes need a different spec

  • Surfaces: two-story custom stucco, slump block, and natural wood such as fascia, beams, garage doors, and gates. Each surface gets its own prep, and wood accents get custom refinishing.
  • Sun exposure: hillside and foothill lots take extreme west-face UV, so product and color choice matter more here than almost anywhere in the Valley. See exterior paint colors that hold up in Arizona heat.
  • Hairline cracking is common on adobe-style and custom stucco. Permanizer's flexibility handles hairline movement, and we repair larger cracks before painting.
  • Access: we use boom lifts for tall two-story elevations and hard-to-reach facades.
  • Protection: pools, stone patios, and landscape installations are fully protected throughout the job.

Our exterior process

  1. Walkthrough with a documented prep scope
  2. Pressure wash
  3. Scrape and sand loose paint
  4. Repair cracks in stucco, block, and wood
  5. Caulk joints and penetrations
  6. Prime bare surfaces
  7. Mask glass and hardscape and protect the pool and landscaping
  8. Apply two full coats of Pittsburgh Paints Permanizer (Benjamin Moore Aura, Sherwin-Williams Emerald, or Dunn-Edwards on request)
  9. Do a final punch-list walkthrough

Two-story homes typically take 3–6 days. Large estates and heavy-prep jobs can take longer, and we give you a realistic timeline before we start. See our full exterior process →

Paradise Valley FAQ

Do you work with my HOA's color palette? Yes. We match colors to your community's approved palette and can submit the HOA color approval for you.

Which paint do you use on stucco and slump block? Premium exterior coatings. Our primary product is Pittsburgh Paints Permanizer, and we use Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, or Dunn-Edwards on request.

How long will an exterior paint job last? Typically 7–12 years with proper prep, backed by our 9-year exterior warranty.

Can you handle the job while I'm away? Yes. We regularly work with absentee and seasonal owners and their property managers. For exteriors we only need gate access, and we keep you or your manager updated from walkthrough to final punch list.

Are you licensed? Yes. ROC #360099. Look it up at roc.az.gov. We're licensed and insured.
