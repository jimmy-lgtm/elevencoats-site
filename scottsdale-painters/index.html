What Scottsdale's climate does to paint and how we plan for it

  • West- and south-facing stucco takes the most UV, so fading and chalking show up there first. We check for chalk before quoting, because paint won't bond over a chalky surface.
  • North Scottsdale hillside lots get more wind-driven grit, and monsoon rain hits north and east elevations harder. Low-slope tile parapets and exposed fascia usually fail first, so we always budget prep time for them.
  • We repair stucco cracks before painting. Paint doesn't fix cracks. Read why stucco cracks and what to do before you paint.
  • Timing: October through April is ideal (50–90°F). We keep painting through the summer by starting early and staying out of direct sun. During monsoon season (July–September) we work mornings and never paint wet surfaces. If weather interrupts a job, we pick it up the next clear morning at no extra charge.

Painting services in Scottsdale

Exterior painting

Scottsdale exteriors are mostly stucco and masonry, with stained wood accents like garage doors, beams, and shutters. Our process: 1. Pressure wash 2. Scrape and sand loose paint 3. Repair cracks and holes in stucco, masonry, and wood 4. Caulk penetrations and joints 5. Spot-prime bare substrate 6. Mask trim, glass, and hardscape 7. Apply two full coats of Pittsburgh Paints Permanizer (Sherwin-Williams Emerald, Benjamin Moore, or Dunn-Edwards on request) 8. Do a punch-list walkthrough

Most single-story exteriors take 2–4 days. Two-story homes typically take 3–6 days. See our full exterior process →

Scottsdale painting FAQ

How often does a Scottsdale exterior need repainting? About every 7–12 years with quality paint and proper prep. Cheap paint or a poor application can fail in 3–5 years.

Which paint do you use? Our primary exterior product is Pittsburgh Paints Permanizer, with Acrishield Max as a step-down option. We also use Sherwin-Williams Emerald and Duration, Benjamin Moore, and Dunn-Edwards Evershield, and we can match a color you've already picked.

Do I need to be home? Not for exteriors, as long as we have gate access. For interiors, most clients are home on day one to confirm colors.

Can you work with my HOA's palette? Yes. Color-board matching is included.

Do you give phone quotes? No. Every estimate is free, in person, and in writing. We schedule the walkthrough within 48 hours of your call and deliver the written quote within 24 hours of it.

What does the warranty cover? Exteriors carry a 9-year labor-and-materials warranty against peeling, cracking, blistering, or failure caused by application or materials. It doesn't cover impact damage, water intrusion from structural problems, or normal UV fading. Cabinets carry a 2-year warranty.

Are you licensed? Yes. ROC #360099. We're licensed and insured.
