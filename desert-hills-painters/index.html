Painters for Desert Hills Custom and Ranch Homes

Desert Hills isn't a subdivision. It's an unincorporated Maricopa County community of acreage homes, horse properties and one-off custom houses, tucked between Phoenix, Cave Creek and Anthem. Painting here means dealing with long driveways, outbuildings, wide-open sun exposure, and homes no two of which are alike. What makes Desert Hills different

The community began in 1955, when a developer subdivided part of the old Sam Joy Ranch into 10-acre parcels. That pattern still shapes it today. Most of the area is zoned for large rural lots, and many homes sit on an acre or more with room for horses, RVs and workshops.

Local coverage notes that most of Desert Hills has no homeowners' association, which is why the architecture varies so much from one lot to the next. You'll see ranch homes, Southwest and territorial styles, contemporary desert builds, and everything in between, set against Gavilan Peak and the foothills around Cave Creek Regional Park.

For you, that means more freedom over color. It also means every scope is different, so we don't use cookie-cutter estimates. We walk the main house and any outbuildings you want painted, and write the scope around what's actually there.

Exterior painting for acreage homes

On a large, open lot, a home takes sun from every side and usually has more exterior surface than a tract house of the same size. Our exterior work includes: - Stucco: wash, open and fill cracks, caulk, and spot-prime before any finish coat. - Wood and trim: scrape, sand and prime fascia, beams, gates and doors. - Outbuildings: casitas, garages, barns and workshops can be added to the same scope. - Height: tall elevations, high gables and steep sites get boom lift access where the ground allows it.

Exterior projects carry our 9-year warranty. More detail is on our exterior painting page.

Walkthroughs built for big properties

A Desert Hills walkthrough takes longer than a tract-home visit, and it should. We walk the full perimeter and note how far equipment has to travel from the driveway. We also flag any wash crossings or soft ground that affect where a lift can safely set up. Then we confirm which structures you want included. Your written scope lists each building separately, so nothing gets missed or assumed.

HOA or no HOA

Most Desert Hills owners won't need an HOA approval at all. A handful of gated or planned pockets do have associations. If yours does, we prepare and submit the color approval for you before any work starts.

Interiors

Cabinets

Painting cabinets can refresh a ranch-house kitchen without a remodel: - Doors are sanded on site. - Oak is grain-filled. - A thin shellac primer keeps stain and tannins from bleeding through. - You choose any sheen from low sheen to gloss.

Cabinet work carries a 2-year warranty. See our cabinet painting page and our stained-to-white kitchen project.

Second homes and owners who travel

If the property is a seasonal home or a rental, we work with absentee owners and property managers. We coordinate gate access and send updates so you don't have to be there.

Desert Hills painting FAQ

Do you work outside Phoenix city limits? Yes. Desert Hills is unincorporated Maricopa County, and it's part of our regular service area.

Can you paint my barn, casita or workshop too? Yes. We include outbuildings in the same walkthrough and scope.

I don't have an HOA. Can you help me choose colors? Yes. We'll talk through options at the walkthrough. If you do have an association, we'll handle the approval.

My home is two stories on a slope. Can you reach it? High and hard-to-reach elevations are handled with boom lift work where the site allows.
