Desert Hills Painters for Custom and Ranch Homes

Desert Hills isn't a subdivision. It's an unincorporated Maricopa County community of acreage homes, horse properties and one-off custom houses, tucked between Phoenix, Cave Creek and Anthem.

Painting a property like this usually means a home set well back from the road, barns or other outbuildings, and walls with little shade, on lots where no two homes are alike. Eleven Coats Painting is licensed and insured (ROC 360099), and we'll walk your property within 48 hours of your call.

What makes Desert Hills different

The community began in 1955, when a developer subdivided part of the old Sam Joy Ranch into 10-acre parcels.

Rural-43 zoning. County planning reports for Desert Hills cite Rural-43, Maricopa County's rural district with a minimum lot of one acre (43,560 square feet) and a minimum width of 145 feet. Maricopa County's area plan for Desert Hills describes homes on lots of one acre or more, set back from the road and often screened by mature desert vegetation.

Horse country. The same plan notes that many Desert Hills lots have barns and pastures for horses, cattle and other ranch animals. It also says riders on the shoulders of unpaved roads and in natural washes are a common sight. County planners describe the area as still made up mostly of rural estates with an equestrian focus, where residents have built small ranchettes.

Mostly no HOA. Local news coverage notes that most of Desert Hills isn't governed by a homeowners' association, which is why the architecture varies so much. You'll find ranch homes and custom desert homes side by side, with views of Gavilan Peak.

For you, that means more freedom over color. It also means every scope is different, so we don't use cookie-cutter estimates. We walk the main house and any outbuildings you want painted, and write the scope around what's actually there.

Exterior painting for acreage homes

In our experience, a home on an open lot with little shade or neighboring structures takes direct sun on every elevation, and sun-facing walls tend to fade and chalk first. Ranch properties also tend to have more exterior surface than the main house alone, once barns, stalls and casitas are counted. Our exterior work includes:

  • Stucco: wash, open and fill cracks, caulk, and spot-prime before any finish coat.
  • Wood and trim: scrape, sand and prime fascia, beams, gates and doors.
  • Outbuildings: casitas, garages, barns and workshops can be added to the same scope.
  • Height: tall elevations, high gables and steep sites get boom lift access where the ground allows it.

Exterior projects carry our 9-year warranty. More detail is on our exterior painting page.

Walkthroughs built for big properties

A Desert Hills walkthrough takes longer than a tract-home visit, and it should. We walk the full perimeter and note how far equipment has to travel from the driveway. We also flag any wash crossings or soft ground that affect where a lift can safely set up. Your written scope lists each building separately, so nothing gets missed or assumed.

HOA or no HOA

Most Desert Hills owners won't need an HOA approval at all. A few planned or gated neighborhoods have associations, such as one gated subdivision approved by the county in 2025. If yours does, we prepare and submit the color approval for you before any work starts.

Desert Hills painting FAQ

Do you work outside Phoenix city limits? Yes. Desert Hills is unincorporated Maricopa County, and it's part of our regular service area.

Can you paint my barn, casita or workshop too? Yes. We include outbuildings in the same walkthrough and scope.

I don't have an HOA. Can you help me choose colors? Yes. We'll talk through options at the walkthrough. If you do have an association, we'll handle the approval.

My home is two stories on a slope. Can you reach it? High and hard-to-reach elevations are handled with boom lift work where the site allows.

Is your crew licensed? Yes. We're licensed and insured under ROC 360099.
