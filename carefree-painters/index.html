Carefree House Painters for Custom Desert Homes

Carefree was planned around the desert rather than in spite of it. Homes sit among granite boulders and saguaros below Black Mountain, and the town's first golf course was routed straight through native desert. Painting a Carefree home means choosing colors that belong in that landscape and protecting it while the work gets done. Eleven Coats Painting is licensed and insured (ROC 360099). Where we paint in Carefree

  • The Boulders: homes set among the giant granite formations that gave the community, the resort and the golf club their name.
  • Desert Forest: custom homes around Desert Forest Golf Club. The course opened in 1962 as the first desert golf course in the Southwest.
  • Black Mountain Foothills: homes on the slopes below Black Mountain, near the county's Black Mountain Summit Preserve.
  • Carefree Rolling Hills, Sentinel Rock Estates and SkyRanch at Carefree: established custom-home neighborhoods. SkyRanch sits near the town's airport, which opened in 1961.
  • Town Center: homes near the Carefree Sundial and Carefree Desert Gardens.

Painting that fits the desert

Carefree homes are mostly custom-built, and many are designed to recede into the landscape. That makes color choice and surface prep the whole job. During the walkthrough we: - Look at how each elevation faces the sun and the views. - Check every stucco and wood surface. - Note any boulders, native plantings and hardscape that need protecting during the work.

If your neighborhood has an association with design guidelines, we prepare and submit the HOA color approval for you before the job is scheduled.

Choosing colors for a Carefree home

Many owners here want a home that blends into the granite, the desert soil and the green-gray of the native plants rather than standing out against them. We bring that up early. At the walkthrough we look at your setting and talk through body, trim and accent combinations that suit it, and our post on exterior colors that hold up in Arizona is a good starting point. Once you've chosen, the approval paperwork, if your neighborhood needs it, is on us.

Exterior painting and stucco

Our exterior process: - Clean the surfaces. - Repair stucco cracks and caulk joints. - Prime bare and patched areas. - Mask glass, stone and hardscape.

Wood details common on Carefree homes, such as beams, fascia, gates and garage doors, are scraped, sanded and primed before finishing. On hillside lots and tall elevations, we use boom lift access where the ground allows it.

Exterior work is backed by our 9-year warranty. Exterior painting →

Interiors, doors and built-ins

Custom homes come with custom interiors: tall ceilings, built-in shelving and plenty of solid wood doors. We paint walls, ceilings, trim and doors with floors and furnishings fully protected. For a look at dark built-ins and interior doors, see our Paradise Valley doors and built-ins project.

Cabinet painting

For kitchen and bar cabinets: - Doors are sanded on site. - Oak is grain-filled before priming. - A thin shellac primer stops stain bleed-through. - Sheens run from low sheen through eggshell, satin and semi-gloss to gloss.

Cabinet work carries a 2-year warranty. Cabinet painting →

Seasonal homes

A lot of Carefree homes sit empty part of the year. We work with absentee owners and property managers, so the walkthrough, the HOA approval and the painting itself can all happen while you're away. You'll get updates until the job is signed off.
