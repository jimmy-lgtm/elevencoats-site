Scottsdale: Espresso Shaker Cabinets to White, Plus a Full Interior Repaint

Summary

For a Scottsdale home, we painted dark espresso shaker kitchen and bar cabinets white and repainted the interior, including walls, ceilings, and doors.

The challenge

  • The full kitchen of espresso shaker cabinets ran from floor-to-ceiling pantry towers around a built-in refrigerator, to uppers over the range, to a separate bar wall with glass-front uppers and beverage coolers.
  • Doing the cabinets and a whole-interior repaint at the same time meant heavy masking throughout the home.

What we did

  • Cabinets: we painted the kitchen and bar cabinets from espresso to white.
  • Interior repaint: we repainted the walls, ceilings, and doors.
  • Protection: we masked and protected the finished surfaces, appliances, and floors throughout.

The result

A bright white shaker kitchen with granite counters and stainless appliances, and a bar wall where the white glass-front uppers now blend into the freshly painted walls.
