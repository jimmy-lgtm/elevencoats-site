How exterior approval works in Desert Ridge

The Desert Ridge Community Association (DRCA) is the master association for the area. According to the Aviano community's own FAQ, homeowners must submit an Architectural Approval Request Form to DRCA before starting any exterior work on their home or yard. A sub-community may have its own association layered on top.

That's why we treat the approval as part of the job:

  • We help you pick a scheme from the colors your association allows.
  • We prepare and submit the color approval request for you.
  • We schedule paint only after approval comes back.

You don't have to chase forms, and you won't get a violation letter for starting too early.

Neighborhoods we paint in Desert Ridge

  • Aviano at Desert Ridge: a Toll Brothers community where DRCA handles exterior modification review.
  • The Villages at Aviano: a separately managed sub-community inside Aviano.
  • Fireside at Desert Ridge: a gated community with its own clubhouse.
  • Sanctuary at Desert Ridge: one of Desert Ridge's newer neighborhoods, a gated community of single- and two-story homes that Taylor Morrison built between 2015 and 2017, with trails and a clubhouse.
  • Neighboring Sky Crossing: a separate master-planned community with its own design review committee, which requires approval of color schemes and exterior finishes before work begins. We prepare that submission too.
  • Other Desert Ridge neighborhoods in the Desert View Village area around Tatum Boulevard and the Loop 101.

Desert Ridge homes are production-built, so a street often has many homes from the same builder palette. Color matching and consistent sheen across body, trim and accents matter more here than in a custom-home area.

Exterior painting and stucco

Most Desert Ridge exteriors are stucco. Before any color goes on, we:

  • Wash the surfaces and clean off dust and chalk.
  • Open up and fill stucco cracks so they don't show through the new coat.
  • Caulk joints and prime bare or patched areas.
  • Protect landscaping, pavers and pool decks.

Two-story elevations and high gables are handled with boom lift work where the site allows it, so trim and fascia get the same attention as the walls you can reach from the ground. Exterior work carries our 9-year warranty.

Interior painting

Inside, we repaint walls, ceilings, trim and doors with the furniture and floors fully protected. That includes tall great rooms, which are common in two-story Desert Ridge floor plans. We confirm color placement with you on day one and walk the finished rooms before we call the job done.

Cabinet painting

For kitchens and baths:

  • We sand cabinet doors on site.
  • If your cabinets are oak, we grain-fill them so the open grain doesn't telegraph through the new color.
  • We block stain and tannin bleed-through with a thin shellac primer.
  • You choose the sheen, anywhere from low sheen through eggshell, satin and semi-gloss to gloss.

Cabinet work carries a 2-year warranty. See how this looks in practice in our espresso slab cabinets painted white project, and read more on our cabinet painting page.

Rentals, second homes and property managers

DRCA limits leases to a minimum of six months, so many Desert Ridge homes are longer-term rentals or seasonal residences. We regularly work with absentee owners and property managers:

  • One point of contact for access and scheduling.
  • The HOA approval handled on your behalf.
  • Updates from walkthrough to final punch list.

Desert Ridge painting FAQ

Do I need HOA approval before repainting my Desert Ridge home? Yes. DRCA requires an Architectural Approval Request before exterior work begins, and we prepare and submit it for you.

I live in Aviano. Who reviews my colors? Aviano's own FAQ points exterior modifications and architectural review to DRCA, the master association. We confirm the right path during the walkthrough.

I'm out of state. Can you paint my rental? Yes. We work with absentee owners and property managers every week and keep you updated throughout.

How fast can you come out? Walkthroughs are scheduled within 48 hours of your call. Our phones are answered live, 24/7.

Are you licensed? Yes. Eleven Coats Painting is licensed and insured under Arizona ROC 360099. Jim Bailey has run painting companies since 1996 and founded Eleven Coats Painting in 2019.
