Where we paint north of the 101

Desert Ridge The City of Phoenix's plan for Desert View Village describes Desert Ridge as the village's master-planned core, served by the Loop 101 and SR-51, with Mayo Clinic hospital nearby. The Desert Ridge Community Association is the master association, and it requires an architectural approval request before exterior work. Neighborhood-level detail is on our Desert Ridge page.

Norterra On the west side, Norterra is a master-planned, mixed-use area of roughly 400 acres in the city's Deer Valley Village, north of the 101 along I-17 next to The Shops at Norterra. Its residential community, Union Park at Norterra, is one of the newer neighborhoods in this part of Phoenix.

Tatum Ranch Tatum Ranch is a master-planned community about five miles north of the 101, inside Phoenix city limits but with Cave Creek mailing addresses. Its community association describes roughly 3,400 homes in 30 neighborhoods from a range of builders, three of them gated, all built out by the end of 2001. Homes that are 20-plus years old, most of them stucco, often need careful prep before a repaint.

Anthem Anthem isn't part of Phoenix. It's an unincorporated master-planned community in Maricopa County, just north of Phoenix along I-17, founded by Del Webb in 1999. The Anthem Community Council notes that its homes fall under three homeowners' associations: Parkside, Country Club and The Village. Parkside west of I-17 is served by the City of Phoenix, while the east side falls under the county. Knowing which HOA you belong to is the first step in any exterior repaint.

Heading toward the foothills? See our Cave Creek, Carefree and Desert Hills pages. South of the 101, our focus is Scottsdale and Paradise Valley.

HOA approvals, handled

In a master-planned community, the paperwork can hold a project up as long as the painting does. We help you choose from your association's approved colors, then prepare and submit the HOA color approval for you. We schedule the work only after approval comes back. If your home sits under more than one association, such as a sub-association below a master association, we check which approval your neighborhood requires before submitting. Read our HOA repaint bidding guide for more.

Exterior painting and stucco

Our exterior scope covers:

  • Washing every elevation.
  • Opening and filling stucco cracks so they don't telegraph through the finish.
  • Caulking windows, joints and penetrations.
  • Priming bare and patched areas.
  • Masking glass, pavers and landscaping.

Cabinet painting

Our cabinet process:

  • Doors are sanded on site.
  • Oak is grain-filled so the grain doesn't print through a solid color.
  • A thin shellac primer blocks stain and tannin bleed-through.
  • Sheens run from low sheen to gloss.

Cabinet work carries a 2-year warranty. See our cabinet painting page, plus two kitchens we've done: espresso shaker cabinets to white and espresso slab cabinets to white.

North Phoenix painting FAQ

Which areas does this page cover? North of the Loop 101: Desert Ridge, Norterra, Tatum Ranch and Anthem. South of the 101, we focus on Scottsdale and Paradise Valley.

Is Anthem part of Phoenix? No. Anthem is an unincorporated master-planned community in Maricopa County, just north of Phoenix. We paint there the same way we do inside city limits.

Will you submit my HOA color approval? Yes. We prepare and submit it for you, then schedule once it's approved.

My Tatum Ranch home has never been repainted. What's involved? Usually washing, stucco crack repair, caulking and spot-priming before two finish coats. We document the prep scope at the walkthrough.

I'm a landlord and don't live nearby. Can you still do the job? Yes. We work with absentee owners and property managers.
