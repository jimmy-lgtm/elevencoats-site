The North Phoenix areas we serve

The City of Phoenix splits north Phoenix into planning "villages," each with a very different housing mix.

North Mountain Village: Moon Valley and Sunnyslope The city describes this area as established neighborhoods plus homes in the foothills of the Phoenix Mountains, with a wide mix of housing types and lot sizes. - Moon Valley grew up around Moon Valley Country Club. - Sunnyslope is one of the city's best-known older neighborhoods.

Older homes here often need more prep than paint. Expect years of previous coats, patched stucco, and wood trim that needs scraping and priming before it can hold a finish.

Deer Valley Village: Norterra and the I-17 corridor The city calls Deer Valley an active area for single-family homes, set against desert and mountains. Neighborhoods around The Shops at Norterra and the Deer Valley Rock Art Center are typically newer subdivisions with HOA color rules.

Desert View Village: Tatum Ranch, Tatum Highlands, Dove Valley Ranch Farther north, the city's plan maps Tatum Ranch, Tatum Highlands and Dove Valley Ranch in a suburban-desert band, with rural horse properties to the northeast. Desert Ridge is also in this village. It has its own master association, covered on our Desert Ridge page.

Exterior painting and stucco repair

North Phoenix is a stucco town, and stucco only looks as good as the repair under it. Our exterior scope covers: - Washing the walls. - Opening and filling cracks so they don't reappear through the new coat. - Caulking windows and penetrations. - Spot-priming any bare or patched areas. - Full masking of glass, hardscape and landscaping.

Where homes have tall elevations, the work is done with boom lift access. Exterior projects carry our 9-year warranty. Read more on our exterior painting page or our guide to stucco cracks before painting.

HOA repaints

Many Deer Valley and Desert View subdivisions are run by HOAs with approved color palettes. We prepare and submit the HOA color approval for you, then schedule once it's approved. For multi-home or community repaint bids, see our HOA repaint bidding guide.

Kitchen and bath cabinets

Painting cabinets is one of the most common upgrades we do in older North Phoenix kitchens: - Doors are sanded on site. - Oak gets grain-filled so the grain doesn't show through. - A thin shellac primer blocks old stain from bleeding into a light color. - Finishes run from low sheen through eggshell, satin and semi-gloss to gloss.

Cabinet work is backed by a 2-year warranty. See our cabinet painting page and a recent dark-stained to white kitchen.

Rentals and absentee owners

North Phoenix has plenty of rental homes and winter residences. We work directly with absentee owners and property managers. We coordinate access, handle the HOA submission, and send updates until the final punch list is signed off.

North Phoenix painting FAQ

Which parts of North Phoenix do you cover? Moon Valley, Sunnyslope, Deer Valley, the Norterra area, Tatum Ranch, Tatum Highlands, Dove Valley Ranch and Desert Ridge, plus neighboring areas in between.

My house is older and the stucco is cracked. Can you still paint it? Yes. Crack repair and priming are part of our exterior scope, and we document that prep during the walkthrough.

Will you deal with my HOA? Yes. We prepare and submit the color approval for you.

I don't live in the house. Is that a problem? No. We regularly work with absentee owners and property managers.

How soon can I get a walkthrough? Within 48 hours of your call.
